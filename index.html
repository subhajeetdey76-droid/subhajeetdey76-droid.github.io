DCM moves less than the market (β of 0.77), but almost all of its risk is company-specific: only about 4% of its variance comes from the Sensex. Returns beat CAPM by a wide margin in 2021 and 2024 and fell short in 2022, 2023 and 2025. A diversified investor is paid for very little of the volatility this stock carries.

  • Pulled five years of daily OHLC, volume and delivery data from BSE for DCM Ltd. and the Sensex, then aligned the two series by date.
  • Calculated daily returns, average return, standard deviation, covariance, correlation and beta with live Excel formulas. Each figure sits next to its formula and a written interpretation.
  • Annualised the returns and computed CAPM expected returns year by year, comparing each year with the actual return.
  • Built a two-asset minimum-variance portfolio of the stock and the market.

Is Delta's 83.46% on-time claim true?

The annual claim holds: 82.54% is well within normal sampling error of 83.46%. Performance is not even through the year, though. Summer runs significantly below the claim and fall significantly above it, and both results survive a Bonferroni correction.

  • My part: showed that arrival delays are far from normal (median −8 min, mean +3.5 min, longest delay 522 min), so the analysis models the on-time count as binomial rather than using the minutes.
  • Checked the normal-approximation conditions (np = 1,204 and n(1−p) = 239). The exact binomial probability came within 1 percentage point of the approximation.
  • Showed where a normal model would mislead: it puts 9.3% of flights over an hour late, but the actual figure is 6.5%.
  • The team cross-checked every figure with a second method in the validation sheet, and all checks matched.

Movie booking database in MySQL

Engine MySQL 8 · MySQL WorkbenchSchema 5 related tables with primary and foreign keysScope DDL, DML, filtering, aggregation, joins

How do you store movies, theatres, shows, customers and bookings so that one query can tell you who watched what, where, and how much they paid?

Entity relationship diagram

Primary keys (PK) in green, foreign keys (FK) in amber. Customer reaches Movie only through Booking and ShowDetails.

Four-table join: every booking, end to end

Booking → Customer, ShowDetails → Movie, Theatre

SELECT c.CustomerName, m.MovieName, t.TheatreName, t.City,
       s.ShowDate, s.TicketPrice, b.SeatsBooked, b.TotalAmount
FROM Booking b
JOIN Customer c    ON b.CustomerID = c.CustomerID
JOIN ShowDetails s ON b.ShowID = s.ShowID
JOIN Movie m       ON s.MovieID = m.MovieID
JOIN Theatre t     ON s.TheatreID = t.TheatreID;
